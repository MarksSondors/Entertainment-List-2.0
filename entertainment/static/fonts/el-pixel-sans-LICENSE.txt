Based on the "MS Sans Serif" FontStruction by lou (2017), as shipped with
98.css (https://github.com/jdan/98.css), licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

Changes, 2026: added Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A letters (accents built on the
font's 11-pixel grid), curly quotes, dashes, ellipsis, middle dot, bullet,
arrows, triangles, check, close, star and a few other UI symbols; completed the
bold (98.css's bold has no , . ' / ? and similar) by the same rule the original
bold follows (each glyph OR'd with itself one pixel to the left); spaced the
regular to the advance widths the site already used, so existing text keeps
its layout.

These font files are distributed under the same licence.
